**The journey may have reached its closing chapter, but the transformation continues.**

On Saturday, 7 November 2026, the Jacob Chidimma Transformation and Empowerment Foundation (JCTEF) officially brought its first Transformation Programme cohort to a close with an end-of-programme meetup for fellows in the Lagos Chapter at JJT, Ikeja, Lagos.

The gathering gave fellows the opportunity to reconnect beyond the virtual learning environment, reflect on their three-month journey, share their experiences, and celebrate the connections and relationships built throughout the programme.

## Reflecting on the journey

One of the highlights of the meetup was the programme review and experience-sharing session.

Fellows had the opportunity to reflect on their participation, discuss lessons learned, share personal experiences, and highlight how the programme had influenced their perspectives and personal development.

Beyond the sessions and learning activities, the conversations provided an opportunity to appreciate the different experiences, challenges and growth journeys of the people who made up the first cohort.

It was a reminder that transformation is not measured only by what we learn, but also by how we begin to think differently, approach opportunities, relate with others, and take responsibility for our growth.

## Building relationships beyond the classroom

The meetup also featured a networking session, giving fellows the opportunity to interact, connect and strengthen relationships beyond the structured programme sessions.

There were also moments of laughter, relaxation and fun as fellows enjoyed spending time together in person.

For a cohort that had spent much of its learning journey connecting virtually, being able to meet physically, interact and build friendships added another valuable dimension to the experience.

## From beneficiaries to contributors: continuing the vision through service

Perhaps one of the most important conversations from the meetup was about what comes next.

As JCTEF prepares for subsequent cohorts of the Transformation Programme, fellows were encouraged to see themselves not merely as participants who have completed a programme, but as individuals who can contribute to the growth and development of others.

They had the opportunity to volunteer to share their experiences with incoming fellows, support future programme activities, and contribute to the broader vision of JCTEF.

This reflects an important value that JCTEF seeks to cultivate: **the spirit of service and the willingness to contribute to the development of others.**

We believe that empowerment should not end with the individual. As people discover their potential, develop their capacity and gain access to opportunities, they can also become part of the process of creating opportunities for others.

The goal is to build a community where people do not only benefit from transformation but also become active contributors to it.

## Looking ahead

The successful conclusion of Cohort 1 marks an important milestone in JCTEF's commitment to human capital development and transformation.

The Transformation Programme was designed to give young people a foundation for personal growth, capacity development and economic participation through a structured three-month learning journey.

While this cohort has officially concluded, the relationships, lessons, experiences and opportunities for continued growth remain.

We sincerely appreciate every fellow who committed to the journey, every facilitator who generously shared their knowledge and experience, and everyone who contributed to making the first cohort possible.

To our fellows: thank you for being part of this beginning. We encourage you to keep learning, growing, connecting and contributing. Your journey does not end here.

**At JCTEF, we believe that potential alone is not enough. Potential must be discovered, developed, disciplined and directed.**

As we continue to pursue our vision of transforming and empowering young people, we remain committed to building individuals who can create value, access opportunities and make meaningful contributions to society.

**Transformation precedes empowerment. Human transformation creates economic transformation.**

*Here's to the beginning of many more stories of growth, transformation and impact.*
