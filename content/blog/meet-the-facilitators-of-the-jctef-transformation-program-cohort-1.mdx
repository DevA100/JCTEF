## Meet the Facilitators: JCTEF Transformation Program – Cohort 1

Transformation happens when people are equipped with the right knowledge, skills, mindset, and support to take intentional steps forward.

For **JCTEF Transformation Program – Cohort 1**, we have brought together facilitators with diverse experiences and expertise to guide participants through key areas of **personal development, wellbeing, financial literacy, technology, and career growth**.

Meet the facilitators who will be helping our participants **transform, equip, and empower themselves for what comes next.**

---

## 🧠 Tamunoemi Abraham

### Emotional Intelligence & Digital Literacy

Tamunoemi will lead sessions on **Emotional Intelligence and Digital Literacy**, helping participants better understand themselves and others while developing the digital skills needed to navigate today’s increasingly connected world.

Participants will explore practical ways to improve self-awareness, communication, emotional management, and their effective use of digital tools.

---

## 💰 Barr. Abasi-ene Okon Bassey

### Financial Literacy

Financial knowledge is an important part of building a sustainable future.

Barr. Abasi-ene will guide participants through **financial literacy**, helping them develop a better understanding of money, financial decisions, and the principles that can support more intentional financial planning.

---

## 🤝 Ferdinand Ibezim

### How to Sell Your Skills & Solutions

Having valuable skills is one thing. **Knowing how to communicate their value is another.**

Ferdinand will help participants understand how to **position, communicate, and sell their skills and solutions** effectively.

The session will focus on practical approaches to communicating value, understanding people’s needs, and turning expertise into opportunities.

---

## 🌍 Alex Miracle

### Scaling Your Tech Skills Internationally

Alex will take participants beyond simply acquiring technical skills and explore how those skills can be developed and positioned for **international opportunities**.

Participants will gain perspectives on building globally relevant technology skills, navigating international opportunities, and positioning themselves for the global digital economy.

---

## 💻 Peter (Imoleayo) Bakare

### Technical Skill Acquisition

Peter will focus on **technical skill acquisition**, helping participants understand how to identify relevant technical skills, learn effectively, and continuously develop their capabilities.

The goal is to encourage participants to move from simply wanting to acquire skills to developing a practical and intentional approach to learning.

---

## 💚 Okoro Olivia Tochi

### Mental Wellness, Fitness, Self-Discovery & Personal Development

Transformation is not only about career and professional growth. **It also begins from within.**

Olivia will lead sessions focused on **mental wellness, fitness, self-discovery, and personal development**, encouraging participants to pay attention to their wellbeing while developing a deeper understanding of themselves and their potential.

Her sessions will help participants reflect on who they are, where they are going, and the habits and choices that can support healthier and more intentional lives.

---

## More Than a Training Programme

The JCTEF Transformation Program is designed to look at transformation from different angles — **mindset, wellbeing, finances, digital capability, technical skills, and career opportunities.**

Through the experience and perspectives of our facilitators, participants will have the opportunity to learn, reflect, develop practical skills, and connect what they learn to their own goals.

**This is a journey of becoming more equipped for what comes next.**
